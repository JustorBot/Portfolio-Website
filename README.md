## 📖 Overview  
This repository contains the source code for my portfolio website. The website is designed to showcase my skills, highlight my projects, and provide visitors with an easy way to learn more about me and get in touch.

### Goals of the Website:  
- **Highlight Skills**: Present the programming languages, frameworks, and tools I specialize in.  
- **Showcase Projects**: Display my notable projects with detailed descriptions, links, and screenshots.  
- **About Me**: Share my background, achievements, and interests.  
- **Contact Info**: Provide an easy way for potential collaborators, clients, or recruiters to reach out.

---

## ✨ Features  
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first design, ensuring a seamless experience across devices.  
- **Projects Section**: Dynamic cards showcasing projects with links to live demos and repositories.  
- **About Me Section**: A brief bio with a timeline of my career and skills.  
- **Contact Form**: A functional form to send me messages directly.  
- **Interactive Animations**: Subtle animations to enhance user experience.
